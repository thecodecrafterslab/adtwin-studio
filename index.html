Create your twin

  1. Record about 2 minutes of yourself talking to camera (15 seconds minimum).
  2. Confirm it's you by reading a short consent statement on camera. This stops anyone cloning someone else.
  3. Wait a few minutes while your twin trains. Your voice is cloned from the same recording.

Read this aloud while recording

Hi, I'm recording this so I can make video ads with my own digital twin. I'm going to talk for about two minutes, looking straight at the camera, speaking clearly at my normal pace.

Let me tell you a little about what I do. I run a business, and every day I help customers find what they need. I like keeping things simple and honest, and I always try to answer questions the way I'd want someone to answer mine.

When people ask me what makes a good product, I tell them it's the one that solves a real problem without fuss. It should be easy to use, it should last, and it should be worth what you paid for it.

Some days are busy and some are quiet, but I enjoy all of it. I like meeting new people, hearing their stories, and finding the right fit for them. If you've got a question, just ask, and I'll do my best to help.

Thanks for watching. I'm looking forward to sharing more with you soon.

Tips: face a window or lamp, keep your whole face in frame, only you in shot, quiet room. Pause for a second with your mouth closed before you start.

iPhone videos: in Settings › Camera › Formats choose "Most Compatible" so they save as MP4, or record with the camera option above.

Consent rule Only create a twin of yourself. Every twin needs the person to confirm on camera before it can make videos. AdTwin uses the twin in your own ads only.

What powers each step

What each step runs on.

Fetch

Product scraping

Firecrawl or a scraper reads title, price, photos from the link.

Live

Ad copy and scripts

Claude writes headlines, primary text and video scripts per tone and audience, and reads the product photo if you add one.

Images

Image creatives

An image model via fal.ai or Replicate restyles the product photo into ad scenes.

Live

Your HeyGen twin renders the script through the Supabase backend. Your own engine on RunPod can replace it later.

Included with HeyGen/Tavus, or ElevenLabs for extra languages like Fijian or Hindi.

Campaigns
