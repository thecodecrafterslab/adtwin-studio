Privacy Policy

AdTwin Studio · Last updated 10 November 2026

AdTwin Studio ("AdTwin", "we", "us") is run by Code Crafters Lab Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 15984273). This policy explains what we collect when you use AdTwin, why, who we share it with, and how to delete it. Questions: support@thecodecrafterslab.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a securely hashed password.
  • Your ad content: product links, product details and photos you enter, the ad copy and images we generate, and your edits.
  • Twin recordings: if you create a digital twin, the video you record or upload, your consent recording, and the face and voice model made from them. This is biometric data, and we only process it with your explicit consent, which you give when you create a twin.
  • Twin videos: the scripts you render and the resulting videos.
  • Meta advertising data: if you connect Facebook, an access token for the ad accounts and Pages you choose, the campaigns and ads AdTwin creates for you, and their results (such as spend, impressions and clicks).
  • Plan and billing: the plan you choose. Card payments, once offered, are handled by our payment provider; we don't store card numbers.
  • Technical data: basic logs (such as time of request and errors) kept by our hosting providers for security and troubleshooting.

How we use it

  • To run the service: write ad copy, make image ads, train your twin, render videos and, when you ask, publish ads to your Meta ad account.
  • To keep your account secure and your content private to you.
  • To answer support requests and tell you about important changes to the service.

We don't sell your data, we don't use your twin for anyone else's ads, and we don't use your content to train AI models. Our legal bases are performing our contract with you, your explicit consent (for twin recordings), and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

How long we keep it

  • Account details and ads: while your account is open, then deleted within 30 days of closing it.
  • Twins: until you delete the twin or close your account. Deleting a twin removes the face and voice model; we delete the source recordings within 30 days.
  • Meta access tokens: until you disconnect Facebook, remove AdTwin in your Facebook settings, or close your account.
  • Logs: up to 90 days.

Deleting your data

You can delete saved ads and twins yourself in the app (My ads and My twin). To delete your whole account and all its data, including any data received from Facebook:

  1. Email support@thecodecrafterslab.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my AdTwin account".
  2. We confirm by email and delete your account, ads, twins, recordings and Meta tokens within 30 days.

To remove AdTwin's access to Facebook at any time, go to Facebook Settings › Business integrations (or Apps and websites), find AdTwin Studio and choose Remove. Then email us as above if you also want the data we already hold deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, object to or restrict how we use it, and withdraw consent (for example, by deleting your twin). Email us to do any of these. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in the UK that's the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, each account can only read its own records, and service keys are kept on our server, never in your browser. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

AdTwin is for businesses and adults aged 18 or over. We don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app or by email.
